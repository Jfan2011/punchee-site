Punchee

Habit punch cards & rewards, for iPhone

Remember coffee-shop punch cards? Punchee turns your goals into cards you punch. Pick a goal, choose the reward waiting at the end, and punch a hole each time you show up. Read 12 books, then the treat. Twenty workouts, then the new shoes.

Download on the App StoreFree printable punch cards

Why punch cards work

  • You can see your progress. Every punch fills the card a little more.
  • The reward is decided up front. You know exactly what you are working toward.
  • No streaks to break. A missed day doesn't undo a single punch.
  • Do it together. Together cards let a partner, friend or your kids punch the same card.
  • Reminders nudge you at the time you pick.

Free to start

Punchee is free with two cards and every preset theme. Lifetime Core ($19.99, once) or Punchee Plus add unlimited cards, photo cards, AI-designed cards and Solo Adventure.
