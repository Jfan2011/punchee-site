Common questions

My purchase isn't showing

Open Punchee → Store → Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with.

AI designs say they can't be checked

Tap Try Again. If it keeps failing, send us the support code shown under the message.

A design failed partway

You are never charged a credit for a failed design. Reopen the card and use Resume Design; the app picks up where it left off.

I want a refund

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. If a design failed or credits look wrong, email us first and we'll sort it out.

Delete my data

Deleting the app removes everything on your phone. To remove the server-side delivery and purchase records tied to your device, email us with the approximate purchase date; see the Privacy Policy for what we keep.
