Support

Effective October 20, 2026

Email Punchee Support at puncheesupport@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, installed Punchee build, what happened and the support code shown in the app. Never send recovery codes, passwords, signed purchase evidence or payment details.

My purchase or credits are missing

Open Settings → Punchee Store → Restore Purchases. This restores Core entitlement and retries unfinished purchases. Restoring Core does not grant its included designs again. Remaining server credit balances use the separate wallet recovery flow.

Manage or cancel Plus

Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Punchee Plus, or Punchee Store → Manage Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Credits you already received stay in your wallet after the subscription ends.

What Free, Core and Plus include

Free: two active cards and every standard theme. Lifetime Core (one-time) and Plus (monthly or annual): unlimited cards, the Solo Adventure, photo cards and punch icons. Core includes 3 AI credits once; Plus adds 6 AI credits each paid month. Members can buy 10- or 30-credit packs.

I am replacing my phone

On your original phone, open Settings → Privacy → AI Account & Recovery and prepare a private recovery code before replacing the phone. Save it somewhere only you can access. On the replacement phone, redeem that code to regain the same wallet and unsaved designs. The code is a secret and is consumed after successful recovery. Knowing an account ID or having a copied receipt is insufficient. If the original phone and code are already lost, contact support; do not send private purchase evidence by ordinary email.

An AI design was interrupted

Reopen the app and resume the pending design, or check unsaved designs in AI Account & Recovery. An unsaved paid design stays recoverable until saved, explicitly discarded or service-account deletion. Failure of initial artwork creation releases its reservation. An interrupted redo or finalization preserves the existing preview and reserved credit so you can resume. Explicit discard after provider work starts consumes one design credit after a warning. If paid artwork is unexpectedly unavailable, contact support before spending another credit.

I want to stop sharing with OpenAI

Withdraw permission in Settings. You can use bundled designs and saved artwork. Work already sent may finish.

Delete my AI service account

Use Settings → Privacy → AI Account & Recovery to request authenticated deletion and check completion. This deletes service AI content and deactivates linked service access. Local Cards, progress and saved Library artwork remain on your phone. Minimum purchase, refund and recovery records remain to preserve paid value and prevent duplicate grants. Save a private recovery code first if you want to regain remaining credits later.

I want an Apple refund

Apple handles purchases and refunds. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Punchee currently does not send optional Apple consumption responses.
