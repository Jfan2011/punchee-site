AI designs

  • One credit creates one complete design: a finished preview, one optional redo, and the Start and In Progress versions of the image you choose.

Failure of initial artwork creation releases the reservation. An interrupted redo or finalization preserves the existing preview and reserved credit so you can resume. Explicitly discarding after provider work begins consumes the reserved design after a warning; service-account deletion applies the same outstanding-work rule.

Requests undergo validation and safety checks, and the generation instructions call for original imagery. These checks do not guarantee that every prohibited description will be recognized. An initial request rejected before generation does not consume a credit, and failed initial artwork creation releases any reservation; a completed design and an explicitly confirmed discard after work starts follow the credit rules above.
