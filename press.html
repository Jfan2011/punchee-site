Everything you need to write about Punchee

In one sentence

Punchee is an iPhone app that turns goals into punch cards: punch a hole for every step and earn the reward you chose at the start.

In a paragraph

Punchee is the app version of the goal punch cards that took over TikTok at the start of 2026. Each card holds one goal, a set number of punches and a reward at the end, just like a coffee-shop loyalty card. There are no streaks to break, so a missed day never undoes progress. Together cards let couples, friends and families punch the same card, and reminders nudge you at the time you choose. Punchee is made by an independent developer, Jeffrey Afana, and was tested by his own family before launch.
