Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Punchee is intended for people aged 13 and older. It is not intended for children under 13. This age policy does not mean Punchee has verified a user’s age.

Punchee keeps your Cards, progress, reward choices, saved artwork and wellness activity on your phone. Apple may back up phone data according to your device settings. Optional Health and Motion permissions support on-device activities; Punchee does not send those readings to its AI service.

An AI service account is created automatically when your phone registers for secure service access. It uses a random device account identifier and Apple App Attest verification, without a username or password. The service stores purchase and subscription history (product, purchase and renewal dates), credit balances and allocations, refund records, generation status, and security information linked to that identifier and its credit wallet. Apple processes payment; Punchee does not receive your payment-card details.

Before generating or redoing AI artwork, Punchee asks permission to share with OpenAI. Your design subject, chosen style and optional Creative Direction go through the Punchee AI service to OpenAI for safety checking and artwork creation. Generated artwork is also sent to OpenAI when deriving connected stages and checking image safety. Account identifiers, purchase evidence and App Attest evidence go to the Punchee service for verification; these fields are not included in its OpenAI prompts. Cards, progress, your name, photo-library images and Health/Motion readings are not included. Avoid entering private information in AI descriptions. You can decline and use bundled designs, your own photo cards where available, and saved artwork. You can withdraw permission in Settings; work already sent may finish.

Unsaved paid artwork and its design inputs remain recoverable on the service until your phone confirms saving or you explicitly discard the design or delete the service account. Acknowledgment removes server artwork, candidates and input content; minimum generation and financial records remain for delivery, refund and credit reconciliation. Failed requests have their content removed. A private recovery code lets a replacement phone recover the same wallet and unsaved artwork. Save it before replacing your original phone. The code is a secret: anyone holding it can claim the wallet. The server stores its hash, and a successful recovery consumes that code. A receipt or account ID cannot replace it.

You can delete the AI service account in Settings → Privacy → AI Account & Recovery. This deletes service AI content, settles outstanding work and deactivates access for its linked phones. It does not remove local Cards, saved Library artwork or progress, cancel purchases, or initiate an Apple refund. Minimum purchase/refund history, credit allocations and recovery information remain to preserve unused paid value and prevent duplicate grants. Jeffrey Afana reviews the need for these minimum records annually; an annual review does not automatically delete records needed to preserve paid credits or prevent duplicate grants. Public verification-key/counter records remain only to authenticate deletion-status checks; raw attestation receipts and challenges are removed. Save your recovery code privately before deletion if you want to regain remaining credits later. Deletion completion appears in the app; interrupted requests can safely check status again.

Apple refund notifications update credit eligibility. Punchee currently does not send optional Apple consumption responses. Requests for purchase refunds use Apple's normal process.

OpenAI's API documentation says API content is not used for training unless the customer opts in. Default abuse-monitoring retention can include prompts and responses for up to 30 days, with stated exceptions. Deleting content from Punchee does not promise instant removal from OpenAI or infrastructure backups. See OpenAI’s data controls.

Service providers include Render for hosting/database, OpenAI for artwork/safety, and Apple for purchase/device verification. Infrastructure logs may process network addresses and request metadata. Support requests sent to puncheesupport@gmail.com include information you choose to send. Do not send recovery codes or signed purchase evidence by ordinary email. Punchee has no advertising or production analytics integration in the reviewed source. Optional Adventure diagnostics are local opt-in validation in this build.
