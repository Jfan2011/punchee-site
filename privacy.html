Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

The short version. Your cards, punches and progress live on your device. Punchee has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no tracking. The only time anything leaves your phone is when you choose to use an AI design or make a purchase, and we explain exactly what is sent below.

What stays on your device

  • Your cards, goals, punches, streaks, notes, saved artwork and photos you attach to cards.
  • Progress in Calm Cove and other guided activities.
  • Motion & Fitness and Health readings, if you grant access, are read on the device to confirm an activity such as a walk. They are processed locally and are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Widgets on your Home Screen read your card data from the app's shared storage on your device only.

AI designs

When you ask Punchee to paint an AI design, the following is sent to the Punchee server and, from there, to our image provider (OpenAI) to generate the artwork:

  • The goal description you typed, the art style you picked and any optional creative direction.
  • A random account identifier created on your device. It contains no name, email, phone number or Apple ID, and it is used only to keep track of your design credits and deliveries.
  • Device verification data from Apple's App Attest service, which proves the request comes from a genuine copy of Punchee. This does not identify you personally.

Generated images are kept on our server only long enough to deliver them to your phone and to resolve any problem with the delivery, then deleted. The description and style you entered are kept with the design's delivery record for support and purchase reconciliation. We do not use your descriptions or images to train any model. OpenAI processes the request under its API terms and does not use API content to train its models.

Please don't include personal details in a goal description. Descriptions are checked automatically for content that our provider cannot depict.

Purchases

All purchases are made through Apple. We never see your payment details. When you buy Lifetime Core or an AI design pack, Apple's signed purchase receipt is sent to the Punchee server so we can grant your included or purchased designs and honor refunds. If Apple refunds a purchase, Apple notifies our server and any unused designs from that purchase are removed. When Apple asks us about a refund request, we report only whether the purchase was delivered and how much of it was used.

Diagnostics

Punchee does not collect analytics or usage data. If a future version offers optional diagnostics, it will ask for your consent first and you can withdraw it in Settings at any time.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, ad networks or third‑party trackers.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No accounts, passwords or contact lists.

Retention and deletion

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. Server records are limited to the design deliveries and purchase receipts described above, tied to the random identifier. To have those removed, email us from the Support page with the approximate purchase date; we cannot look you up by name because we never have it.

Children

Punchee is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
